# Research Moves

Choose the move that could most change the current explanation. A move is a bounded inquiry, not a permanent review phase.

## Orient

Use when entering an unfamiliar conversation. Locate a recent overview, a foundational work, a current representative study, and a serious dissenting or anomalous work. Produce provisional concepts and tensions. Do not infer coverage from these anchors.

## Concept trace

Use when studies may share a word but not an object. Trace definition, operationalization, measurement, and scope across pivotal sources. Report incompatible comparisons and consequences for the current claims.

## Claim test

Turn a working explanation into a defeasible proposition. Specify supporting and defeating observations. Seek direct evidence and counterevidence separately. Return whether the claim is strengthened, narrowed, rebutted, or still underdetermined.

## Conflict resolve

For an apparent conflict, compare in order:

1. constructs and definitions;
2. outcomes and measurements;
3. population, setting, and scale;
4. time horizon;
5. design, preprocessing, and analysis;
6. statistical uncertainty and power;
7. remaining substantive contradiction.

End with the best supported explanation and the observation that would discriminate remaining alternatives. Do not stop at “the literature is mixed.”

## Citation chase

Trace a consequential claim backward to its primary source and forward to replications, qualifications, or criticism. Check whether many papers merely repeat one evidence source and whether later citations strengthened the original wording.

## Gap probe

Treat “no research exists” as a claim to attack. Search synonyms, neighbouring disciplines, alternate constructs, and negative or null findings. Distinguish an absent literature from an indexing problem, inaccessible evidence, or a question studied under another name.

## Synthesis rebuild

Use when accumulation no longer improves the explanation. Stop searching. Merge equivalent concepts, split overbroad claims, compare competing accounts, convert apparent contradictions into conditional relationships where warranted, and retire frames that no longer explain the evidence. Then regenerate the open tensions.

## Source audit

Use when a draft claim may outrun its citations. Verify exact source support, independence, scope, and whether the cited source is primary. Revise the claim rather than searching for decorative citations.
