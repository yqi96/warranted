# Results

Use this reference for Results prose, figure/table-linked paragraphs, result-bearing captions, and source-data references.

## Result Paragraph Contract

Each result paragraph must be anchored to result propositions.

Before writing the paragraph:

- identify the paragraph proposition
- identify the result propositions that support it
- identify the figure, table, source data, script, or output artifact attached to each supporting proposition
- identify the warrant that licenses the inference from those results to the paragraph proposition

Do not use literature evidence alone to support a Results paragraph proposition.

## Figure And Table Discipline

Figures and tables are evidence interfaces, not arguments by themselves.

- Each result-bearing panel, row, or statistic should map to one or more result propositions.
- Captions must not claim more than the linked propositions establish.
- A visual trend is not enough if the proposition depends on a quantified effect; create or verify the quantitative proposition.
- Source data must be attachable when a figure/table is used as evidence.

## Scope And Strength

If a result comes from a subset, pilot, approximation, exploratory endpoint, failed assumption, or underpowered comparison:

- record the scope in the result proposition, warrant, rebuttal proposition, or unresolved obligation
- do not write an unscoped proposition
- do not let a narrow figure panel support a broad section thesis without a warrant

## Negative And Null Results

Negative, null, or failed results are still evidence.

- If they contradict an expected result or proposition implication, audit the discrepancy before creating a rebuttal.
- If they define a boundary condition, create a rebuttal proposition.
- If they are inconclusive, leave the proposition `unestablished` and name the next obligation.

## Completion

The Results section is complete when:

- every result paragraph has a proposition
- every result proposition is supported by evidence propositions and artifacts
- figure/table captions match the linked propositions
- scoped results remain scoped in prose
- unexpected mismatches are audited before verdict language
