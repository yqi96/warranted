# Introduction And Related Work

Use this reference for Introduction, Background, Related Work, literature review, and any literature-heavy passage.

## Evidence Channel

Apply `literature-writing` for all published-paper evidence.

- Source-specific factual sentences cite evidence propositions with source attachments using `\cite{prop_N}`.
- Paragraph theses are propositions, not citations.
- Synthesis across papers requires warrant text or a promoted warrant proposition.
- Conflicting or boundary-setting literature becomes a rebuttal proposition, not vague hedging.

Do not enumerate papers unless the enumeration serves a proposition.

## Introduction Shape

An Introduction usually advances a chain of propositions:

1. field-level problem or phenomenon
2. known evidence and limitations
3. unresolved gap
4. this manuscript's motivating proposition or question

Each step must be grounded. The gap proposition must not be broader than the cited literature supports.

## Related Work Shape

Related Work should organize evidence by argument function, not by paper order.

Use separate propositions for:

- consensus findings
- method families or conceptual categories
- known limitations
- unresolved disagreement
- the niche this manuscript occupies

A taxonomy or grouping is a proposition unless directly quoted from a source. Support it with evidence propositions and a warrant explaining why the grouping is valid.

## Gap Discipline

Do not write "little is known" or "no studies have..." unless search evidence supports that absence. If the gap is based on literature search, record the search result as evidence or narrow the gap proposition.

If there is contradictory literature, scope the gap proposition rather than making it absolute.

## Completion

The section is complete when:

- every factual paper statement cites a literature evidence proposition
- every synthesis paragraph has a proposition and warrant
- conflicts and limitations are rebuttal propositions
- the stated gap is supported by the cited evidence and search scope
